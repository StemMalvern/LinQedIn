About
Coffee Engineer with nearly five years of experience in agency environments, working across specialty roasteries, cafe fit-outs, and consumer equipment brands. Currently at QinetiQ, where I lead engineering across end-to-end machine builds from concept through to commissioning. I'm particularly interested in the space where thermodynamics and machine design meet — getting the extraction right before the enclosure exists. I care about equipment that is genuinely reliable, not just impressive on a spec sheet. Outside of work I'm involved in animal welfare volunteering with London Paws Rescue, and spend my weekends either tinkering in the workshop, on a slow run around Hackney, or reading something I probably should have read years ago. Always happy to connect with other engineers, roasters, and anyone working on problems worth solving.
